The framework

Organized around the
8-stage lifecycle model

Vendors are categorized by where they actually fit in the post-sale journey — not by marketing labels.

Stage 01

Sign

Capture context, conversations, and handoff signals before ownership fully shifts.

Stage 02

Onboard

Coordinate implementation, onboarding, and time-to-value work across teams.

Stage 03

Activate

Help users reach first value through setup guidance, education, and direction.

Stage 04

Adopt

Track usage, health, and sentiment to improve engagement and adoption.

Stage 05

Support

Improve support delivery with automation, triage, and knowledge access.

Stage 06

Expand

Surface growth opportunities and expansion pathways inside existing accounts.

Stage 07

Renew

Reduce churn risk with better signals, workflows, and renewal forecasting.

Stage 08

Advocate

Turn successful customers into proof through references and advocacy programs.
